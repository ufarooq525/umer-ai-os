# Niche Intelligence

Niches are first-class inputs to Opportunity Intelligence.

The OS must support three ways to operate:

1. **CEO-selected niche** — Umer explicitly tells the OS where to look.
2. **AI-recommended niche** — the OS identifies an attractive market and asks Umer to approve it.
3. **AI-autonomous exploration** — an approved exploration budget lets the OS test multiple niches and promote winners.

## Example: Saudi Umrah Transport

A useful niche hypothesis is:

> Saudi Arabia private Umrah/Hajj transport operators serving international pilgrims who currently acquire customers through Facebook/WhatsApp but lack a strong website, transparent route pricing, online quotation, or advance booking experience.

The opportunity is not simply "build them a website".

The OS should investigate whether a better digital sales system could help the operator:

- publish fixed route prices
- show vehicle capacity and luggage information
- allow airport → hotel bookings
- allow Makkah ↔ Madinah transfers
- collect flight and hotel details
- provide WhatsApp fallback
- collect deposits/payments where appropriate
- reduce manual quote conversations
- improve trust for international customers booking before arrival

- research quota
- minimum opportunity score

```text
IDEA
  ↓

  ↓
TESTING
  ↓
VALIDATED / REJECTED
  ↓
ACTIVE
  ↓
WINNING / DECLINING
  ↓
PAUSED / RETIRED
```

## Important rule

A niche is never considered attractive solely because many businesses exist.

The OS must test:

- business density
- customer pain
- evidence quality
- willingness/ability to pay
- reachability
- competition
- solution fit
- estimated deal value
- sales friction
- delivery feasibility
- observed conversion results
