# Niche Experiment: Saudi Umrah Transport

**Status:** TESTING  
**Geography:** Saudi Arabia  
**Primary customer:** private transport operators serving pilgrims/travelers  
**Primary buyer:** business owner/operator

## Hypothesis

There are transport operators who acquire demand through Facebook, WhatsApp, referrals, or offline travel relationships but do not provide international customers with a clear digital journey for comparing and booking common pilgrimage transfer routes.

## Routes to investigate

- Jeddah Airport → Makkah hotel
- Jeddah Airport → Madinah hotel
- Makkah → Madinah
- Madinah → Makkah
- Madinah → airport
- Makkah → airport
- hotel → airport
- custom/private transfers

The OS must not assume these are the most profitable routes. Research should determine actual demand and pricing.

## Business pain hypotheses

1. Customers must message the operator to ask for price.
2. Customers cannot easily compare vehicle options.
3. International customers have difficulty verifying legitimacy/trust.
4. Operators manually answer repeated route/price questions.
5. Booking details are collected through WhatsApp manually.
6. There is no structured deposit/booking workflow.
7. Operators lose customers who want instant pricing before contacting them.

Each hypothesis requires evidence before being promoted to a validated problem.

## Solution hypotheses

Potential service offers include:

### A. Conversion website

- route/service pages
- fixed or starting prices
- vehicle types
- passenger/luggage capacity
- airport/hotel transfer details
- multilingual content where commercially justified
- WhatsApp CTA
- trust/social proof
- FAQs

### B. Quote + booking system

- pickup/drop-off selection
- date/time
- passengers
- luggage
- vehicle selection
- flight details
- hotel details
- instant/assisted quote
- booking request
- WhatsApp notification
- admin dashboard

### C. Full transport platform

Only consider this if research demonstrates sufficient operational complexity and willingness to pay.

## Competitive research questions

- Who already offers Saudi airport/Umrah transfers online?
- Which companies expose prices publicly?
- Which routes have transparent pricing?
- What vehicle categories are offered?
- Can customers book online?
- Is payment/deposit supported?
- What languages are available?
- What trust signals are visible?
- What are the common customer complaints?
- What differentiates the strongest operators?

## Prospect qualification

Prioritize operators with:

- evidence of active demand
- meaningful reviews/social activity
- multiple vehicles or routes
- professional-looking operation but weak digital sales infrastructure
- clear contact channel
- ability to serve international customers
- signs of repeat/manual inquiries
- reachable owner/decision maker

Avoid treating every Facebook transport page as a qualified lead.

## Guardrails

- Public information only unless a legitimate data connector is authorized.
- Respect platform terms and applicable laws.
- No fabricated prices, reviews, availability, licenses, or claims.
- No mass outreach by default.
- Human approval remains required before commercial outreach in the first version.
