# SEO Audit Workflow

## Objective

Provide a repeatable SEO audit workflow for websites and content systems.

## Stages

1. Collect site structure and pages
2. Review technical SEO signals
3. Review on-page SEO and content quality
4. Check internal linking and crawlability
5. Produce recommendations and priorities

## Inputs

- Target website
- Pages or sitemap
- SEO goals

## Outputs

- Technical findings
- On-page findings
- Priority recommendations
- SEO action plan
