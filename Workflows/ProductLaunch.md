# Product Launch Workflow

## Objective

Deliver a consistent product launch process from planning through verification.

## Stages

1. Pre-launch readiness review
2. Marketing and messaging alignment
3. Technical launch preparation
4. Launch execution
5. Post-launch validation

## Inputs

- Launch brief
- Product goals
- Release timeline

## Outputs

- Launch checklist
- Release readiness notes
- Post-launch review summary
