# Website Redesign Workflow

## Objective

Offer a structure for redesign efforts that balance experience, technical quality, and business outcomes.

## Stages

1. Discovery and audit
2. UX and content planning
3. Design and implementation alignment
4. Launch preparation
5. Review and optimization

## Inputs

- Current website
- Business goals
- UX and SEO constraints

## Outputs

- Redesign brief
- Implementation plan
- Launch readiness checklist
- Performance and quality review
