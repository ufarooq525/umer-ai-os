# Analytics Ten-Year Architecture

Governed by `Departments/Analytics/ANALYTICS_CHARTER.md`.

## Stable Spine

1. Analytics owns measurement systems and insight delivery.  
2. Business Portfolio Analytics owns value-vs-investment judgment.  
3. Engineering Observability owns system health SLOs.  
4. Contexts: Measurement Architecture, Product Analytics, Experimentation, Attribution & Funnel Analytics, Analytics Data Quality.  
5. Event Taxonomy and Metric Dictionary remain decade compounding assets.

## Evolution

Warehouses, vendors, and BI tools change freely under Instrumentation Specs and Dictionary — architecture does not couple to a stack. Marketing (later) consumes Attribution Models; does not fork them.

## Non-Goals

Replacing Business; replacing Intelligence; owning production incident command.
