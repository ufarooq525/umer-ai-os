# Bounded Context: Attribution & Funnel Analytics

Governed by `Departments/Analytics/ANALYTICS_CHARTER.md`.

## Purpose

Model how outcomes attribute across journeys and funnels under explicit assumptions — preparing for Marketing consumption later without giving Marketing ownership now.

## Owns

Attribution Models, Funnel Models.

## Does Not Own

GTM strategy (Commercial Strategy / Marketing later); Design experience architecture.

## Interfaces

Consumes Event Taxonomy; Informs Business and future Marketing; Constrained by Security on identity stitching.

## Primary Artifacts

Attribution Model, Funnel Model.

## Anti-Patterns

Hidden attribution magic; funnels not mapped to Taxonomy; identity stitching against Security rules.
