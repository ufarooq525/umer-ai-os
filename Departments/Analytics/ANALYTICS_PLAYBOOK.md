# Analytics Playbook

Governed by `Departments/DEPARTMENT_ARCHITECTURE_STANDARD.md` §12.

## 1. Mandate

Analytics makes reality measurable under shared definitions so decisions share one measurement plane.

## 2. Placement

Domain Analytics · Capability Analytics & Measurement · Topology Cross-Cutting.

## 3. Enter / Leave

Enter via Execution for system-changing measurement work. Leave as Dictionary/Taxonomy/Specs to Eng and Models/Insights to Business/Executive.

## 4. Contexts

Measurement Architecture foundations → Product Analytics and Experimentation and Attribution consume them → Data Quality keeps the plane honest.

## 5. Example

Business needs retention proof → Metric Dictionary entry → Instrumentation Spec → Eng emits → Data Quality validates → Insight Artifact → Portfolio Analytics judges vs Business Case.

## 6. Business Traceability

Measurement serves Business questions; Analytics does not invent strategy.

## 7. Inheritance

KPI Framework and Core honesty rules by link.

## 8. Escalation

Metric disputes → CoS + Dictionary; PII → Security; capacity → PM.

## 9. Anti-Patterns

Portfolio capture; Observability capture; shadow metrics; integrity-free experiments.

## 10. Summary

**Analytics owns the measurement plane — Taxonomy, Dictionary, Models, Experiments, Attribution, Quality — while Business judges value and Engineering keeps systems observable.**
