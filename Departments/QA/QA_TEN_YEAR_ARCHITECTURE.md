# QA Ten-Year Architecture

Governed by `Departments/QA/QA_CHARTER.md`.

## Stable Spine

1. QA independence from Engineering remains non-negotiable.  
2. Core owns Quality Standard; QA owns assurance application and evidence.  
3. Security owns control frameworks; QA may execute delegated verification procedures.  
4. Contexts: Quality Strategy & Gates, Verification & Validation, Non-Functional Quality, Release Validation, Quality Intelligence.  

## Evolution

Automation increases; independence does not decrease. Split only if product-line assurance scale demands Teams first, Departments only under DAS Split.

## Non-Goals

Becoming Engineering; becoming Security; owning Execution gate mechanics.
