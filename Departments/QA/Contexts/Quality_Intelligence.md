## Does Not Own

## Interfaces

Consumes Defect Records; produces harvest packages toward Intelligence/Governance; Informs Engineering/Design via themes.

## Primary Artifacts

Defect theme harvest inputs (durable via Intelligence once accepted).

## Anti-Patterns
