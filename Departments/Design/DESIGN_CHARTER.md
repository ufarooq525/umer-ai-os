# Design Department Charter

> Design is the organizational owner of **product experience quality** — how people understand, navigate, and use what the enterprise ships. Design is not Marketing, not Business Customer Strategy, and not Engineering’s UI implementation team.

Governed by `Departments/DEPARTMENT_ARCHITECTURE_STANDARD.md`. Domain: Design (`Enterprise_Architecture/ENTERPRISE_DOMAIN_MAP.md`). Topology: **Pipeline Stage** (upstream of Engineering for experience surfaces).

## Mission

> We exist to make approved product intent legible and usable for real people, by owning experience architecture, interface design, design systems, and design research validation, so that Engineering builds the right surfaces, QA can verify experience quality, and Business intent survives contact with users.

## Purpose

Translate validated customer problems and product direction into coherent experience specifications and a durable design system — the producer Engineering and QA depend on for experience-bearing work.

## Primary Capabilities

| Capability | Notes |
|---|---|
| Visual & UX Design | Registered — Product Delivery; Design is primary Accountable |

## Responsibilities

1. Experience architecture (information architecture, flows, interaction models)  
2. Interface design for product surfaces  
3. Design System (tokens, components, patterns, usage rules)  
4. Design research & validation for experience decisions (consumes Intelligence/Business research; does not fork market research)  
5. Accessibility intent in design artifacts (implementation verification shared with QA)  
6. Design handoff packages that meet Engineering entry contracts  

## Does Not Own

| Temptation | Actual owner |
|---|---|
| Customer segmentation, JTBD, value propositions as business strategy | `Business/Contexts/Customer_Strategy.md` |
| Roadmap and investment | Business / Executive |
| Implementation of UI in production systems | Engineering / Application Delivery |
| Independent release quality authority | QA |
| Brand campaigns and GTM messaging | Marketing (later phase) |
| Organizational research libraries | Intelligence |
| Security UX control policy | Security (constraints); Design applies within them |
