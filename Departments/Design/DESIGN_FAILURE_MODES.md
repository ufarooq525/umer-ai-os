# Design Failure Modes

Governed by `Departments/Design/DESIGN_CHARTER.md`.

## Architectural Risks

| Mode | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Absorbing Customer Strategy | Business value layer polluted; Design invents segments |
| Skipping Design on UX work | Engineering improvises UX; QA cannot verify intent |
| Design System fork in Engineering | Dual visual languages; adoption collapses |
| Research fork vs Intelligence | Parallel truth; evidence rot |
| Pixel theater without flows/errors | Happy-path-only specs; production failure |

## Organizational Risks

Handoff theater (pretty files, incomplete contracts); Design as Marketing creative shop; infinite redesign without Business Intent; zombie Design folder.

## Long-Term Consequences

Incoherent product family; accessibility debt; Engineering slowdown from ambiguous specs; loss of compounding Design System asset.
