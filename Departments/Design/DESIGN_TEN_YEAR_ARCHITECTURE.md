# Design Ten-Year Architecture

Governed by `Departments/Design/DESIGN_CHARTER.md`.

## Stable Spine

1. Design owns experience quality and Design System authority.  
2. Business owns customer strategy nouns; Design specializes into product experience.  
3. Engineering implements; does not fork the Design System.  
4. Four contexts remain: Experience Architecture, Interface Design, Design Systems, Design Research & Validation.  
5. Marketing (later) does not absorb product Design System ownership.

## Evolution

Years 0–2: single Design Department. Years 2–5: optional Teams by product surface. Years 5–10: split only if multi-brand/multi-product Design System governance exceeds one Department’s coherence — DAS Split rules apply.

## Non-Goals

Becoming Brand/Marketing; becoming frontend Engineering; becoming Business research.
