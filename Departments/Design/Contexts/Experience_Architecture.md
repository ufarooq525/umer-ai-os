# Bounded Context: Experience Architecture

Governed by `Departments/Design/DESIGN_CHARTER.md`.

## Purpose

Structure how users move through product capabilities — IA, flows, interaction models — specializing Business journeys into buildable experience models.

## Owns

Experience Architecture Models, Interaction/Flow Specs.

## Does Not Own

Business Journey Maps as strategy artifacts (Customer Strategy); UI pixels (Interface Design); component library (Design Systems).

## Interfaces

Consumes Business intent + Research; produces flows for Interface Design and Engineering.

## Primary Artifacts

Experience Architecture Model, Interaction/Flow Spec.

## Anti-Patterns

Redefining business segments; flows without error/empty states; skipping research on high-risk journeys.
