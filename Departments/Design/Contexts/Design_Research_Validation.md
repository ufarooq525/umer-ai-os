# Bounded Context: Design Research & Validation

Governed by `Departments/Design/DESIGN_CHARTER.md`.

## Purpose

Evidence experience decisions — generative and evaluative research tied to Design outcomes — without becoming Business Market Intelligence or Intelligence’s research library owner.

## Owns

Design Research Records, Usability Validation Reports, disposition of UX findings before ship.

## Does Not Own

Market sizing/competitor repositories (Intelligence/Business); portfolio investment decisions; QA gate Pass/Fail authority.

## Interfaces

Consumes Intelligence/Business evidence; produces validation that Experience/Interface contexts must dispose; feeds lessons to Intelligence harvest.

## Primary Artifacts

Design Research Record, Usability Validation Report.

## Anti-Patterns

Research theater (no decision impact); forking Intelligence; blocking all delivery for unbounded research.
