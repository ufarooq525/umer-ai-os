# Bounded Context: Design Systems

Governed by `Departments/Design/DESIGN_CHARTER.md`.

## Purpose

Own the durable product design language — tokens, components, patterns, contribution rules — the compounding asset across products and years.

## Owns

Design System, Component/Pattern Specs, adoption and contribution rules.

## Does Not Own

Engineering component implementation mechanics (Engineering implements to Spec); Marketing brand campaign systems (later); Business Capability maps.

## Interfaces

Enables Interface Design and Engineering; versioned breaking changes Consult Engineering; CoS audits documentation currency.

## Primary Artifacts

Design System, Component/Pattern Spec.

## Anti-Patterns

Unversioned breaking changes; allowing Engineering to fork; Design System as static poster without contribution path.
