# Design Playbook

Governed by `Departments/DEPARTMENT_ARCHITECTURE_STANDARD.md` §12.

## 1. Mandate

Design makes approved product intent usable, coherent, and verifiable.

## 2. Placement

Domain Design · Capability Visual & UX Design · Topology Pipeline Stage.

## 3. Enter / Leave

Enter via Execution with Business problem context. Leave to Engineering via validated handoff; criteria shared with QA.

## 4. Contexts

Research validates → Experience Architecture structures → Interface Design specifies → Design Systems enables coherence across all.

## 5. Example

Roadmap item for checkout redesign → Research Record on friction → Experience Architecture Model → Interface Specs on Design System → Handoff to Engineering → QA verifies against criteria.

## 6. Business Traceability

“Why this UX?” → Customer Strategy problem + Product Brief via Execution.

## 7. Inheritance

Core/Governance/Execution/Intelligence/EA by link, not restatement.

## 8. Escalation

Unclear problem → Business; feasibility war → CEO; auth/privacy UX → Security.

## 9. Anti-Patterns

Customer Strategy capture; system fork; research fork; pixel theater; Marketing capture.

## 10. Summary

**Design owns experience architecture, interfaces, design systems, and validation — so Engineering builds the right thing and QA can prove it.**
